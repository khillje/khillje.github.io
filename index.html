A personal household display

The household calendar,
at a glance.

Smart Wall is a privately operated smart calendar that brings a household’s schedule and selected home information together on a local display.

Personal home monitoring

With the account owner’s permission, Google Nest access supports periodic camera snapshots and a brief live doorbell view on the household display. These features are for personal household use.

This informational website does not provide access to the calendar, camera footage, or authorization credentials. It has no sign-in or account registration.

Your home stays personal

Learn what the application accesses, what it retains, and how access can be withdrawn in the Smart Wall privacy policy.
